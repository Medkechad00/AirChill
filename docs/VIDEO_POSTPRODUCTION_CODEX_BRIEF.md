A customer explains the problem, and the assistant asks a few follow-up questions to collect the details the team actually needs.

Once everything is clear, it summarizes the request and asks the customer to confirm.

From there, the request becomes available to the team in a single dashboard. Staff can open it, review the information, and update its status as the work progresses.
